KekLocator @VERSION@

Stereo delay with an internal 16-step sequencer and DAW tempo sync.

Choose VST3, Audio Unit and CLAP. Plug-ins install for the current user in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins.

macOS 11 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

Original retains the firmware 1.05 DSP. Extended adds effect locks, groove, wet ducking, sound/pattern banks, A/B and Undo/Redo.

When upgrading from Echolocator Rust, remove the old Echolocator bundles first. Keep one version of each format installed, then rescan or restart your DAW.
